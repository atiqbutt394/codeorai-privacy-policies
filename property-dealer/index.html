Privacy Policy Property Dealer — Android app by CodeOrAI In short • Your property records (sellers, buyers, deals, payments, reminders) are stored only on your phone. We do not run any server and we never receive this data. • No sign-up or account is needed. • The app does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Effective date: 26 November 2026 This privacy policy explains how the Property Dealer app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is published on Google Play by CodeOrAI. 1. Information you enter in the app The app lets you record property listings, seller and buyer details (such as names and phone numbers), deals, payments, reminders and settings. All of this information: • is stored in a local database on your device; • is never sent to us or to any server operated by us; • is not shared with any third party by the app. You are responsible for having permission to store the contact details of the people you record in the app. 2. Information we collect We do not collect any personal or usage information. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it works fully offline. 3. Backups and PDF sharing If you create a backup file or share a deal as a PDF, the file is created on your device and sent only where you choose (for example, to your own storage, email or a messaging app). We do not receive a copy. Files you share with other apps are then handled under those apps' privacy policies. 4. Permissions • Notifications — to show the reminders you set. Reminders are scheduled on your device. 5. Data retention and deletion Your records stay on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Because we do not hold any of your records, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. 6. Children The app is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. 7. Security Your records are protected by your device's own security (such as your screen lock). We recommend keeping a screen lock enabled and storing backup files in a safe place. 8. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. 9.
